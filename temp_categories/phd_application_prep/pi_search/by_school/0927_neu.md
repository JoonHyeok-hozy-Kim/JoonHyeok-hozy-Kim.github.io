#### Hongyang R. Zhang
- Relevance : mid
- [website](https://www.hongyangzhang.com/)
- Topics : grokking/generalization, neural network optimization, learning theory, algorithmic reasoning
- Papers
  - [A Hessian View of Grokking in Mathematical Reasoning](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BfLVCoov0b)
    - Desc.)
      - high weight decay is essential for grokking to occur
      - the training loss is highly unstable and exhibits strong oscillations
      - adding regularization to the Hessian by injecting isotropic Gaussian noise to the weights of the transformer network
        - Why?)
          - Hessian can capture geometric properties of the loss surface, such as the sharpness of local minima
      - find that this combination of high weight decay and Hessian regularization can smooth out the training loss during grokking
  - Noise Stability Optimization for Finding Flat Minima: A Hessian-based Regularization Approach
  - Robust Fine-Tuning of Deep Neural Networks with Hessian-based Generalization Guarantees
